Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 17, 2026

1

Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Grpah Api ("we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, and protects information in connection with our use of the Meta Graph API and related Meta Platform services to manage and publish content to the Facebook Pages that we own and operate.

2

Information we collect

Through our connected application's use of the Graph API, we may access:

  • Facebook Page information, including Page ID, name, and content associated with Pages we manage
  • Access tokens issued by Meta to authenticate our API requests
  • Content we create and publish ourselves — text, images, and captions — to our own Facebook Pages

We do not collect personal information from visitors to our Facebook Pages, and we do not access data belonging to Facebook users who are not affiliated with our business.

3

How we use this information

Information accessed through the Graph API is used solely to:

  • Publish and manage content on Facebook Pages that we own or administer
  • Operate, maintain, and troubleshoot our own content-publishing tools
  • Keep our Pages and application functioning correctly
4

Data storage and security

Access tokens and related credentials are stored securely and are not shared with third parties. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect this information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

5

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share information obtained through the Graph API with third parties, except where required to comply with applicable law or Meta's Platform Terms.

6

Data retention

We retain access tokens and related data only for as long as necessary to operate our content-publishing tools. Access can be revoked at any time through Facebook Business Settings.

7

Children's privacy

Our services are not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

8

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

9

Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at privacy@yourdomain.com.
